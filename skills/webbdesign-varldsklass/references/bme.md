- **Palett**: elfenben sida `#f3eee3`, text grön-svart `#1c2320`, guld `#7d6338` för text (kontrast 4,89:1 mot elfenben) och `#c4a268` för hårlinjer. Tema-originalen: grön-svart `#0a0e0c`, elfenben `#f3eee3`, guld `#c4a268`/`#ecd29a`. Sidan hålls ljus.

## Låst copy (block B, The Experience). Ändra aldrig orden
- Rubrik: **WHERE THE SENSES LEAD, *THE FEELING DEEPENS***
- Intro: The brain can change its own biology, at any age. Science calls it neuroplasticity.
- Not every moment matters equally.
- Under the right conditions, when the senses carry a moment, attention holds it and feeling seals it, the brain learns more deeply, and keeps what it learns.
- Paren: Breathe. Regulate. / Scent. Evoke. / Touch. Embody. / Feel. Amplify. / **Anchor. Direct.** (Annas rättelse 13 okt; "Associate" var fel)
- Five moments. One direction. A mind that finds its way there more easily, every time.
- Slutrad: It may look like magic. Neuroscience calls it long-term potentiation. We call it the window, and the ritual is how we open it.
